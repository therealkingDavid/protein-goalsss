The Egg Guide
7 EGGS DAILY · SAUCES TO MAKE THEM WORK · STYLES TO KEEP IT INTERESTING
Lagos-Available Sauces
🫙
Knorr Liquid Seasoning
✓ Available — most supermarkets, local stores, markets
Your most accessible option. A few drops while cooking completely transforms the eggy taste. Umami-rich. Works with scrambled, fried, boiled. Buy one bottle and keep it by the stove.
🌶️
Local Pepper Sauce / Shito
✓ Available — everywhere in Lagos, markets, roadside
The most Nigerian solution. Bottled pepper sauce or shito works perfectly with eggs. You already know the flavour profile. Cuts the egg taste entirely. Cheap. Everywhere.
🔴
Sriracha
✓ Available — Shoprite, Spar, Market Square Lagos
Hot, tangy, completely transforms eggs. Particularly good with scrambled eggs. The bottle lasts months. Worth buying once from any major supermarket.
🍶
Kikkoman Soy Sauce
✓ Available — Shoprite, Spar, health food stores
A few drops on eggs cuts the taste completely. Umami depth. Works especially well with fried eggs. Asian grocery stores in Lagos also stock it.
🍅
Heinz Chilli Sauce
✓ Available — most supermarkets
Milder than Sriracha. Good for those who want flavour without too much heat. Classic sauce that pairs well with eggs in any style.
Cooking Styles — Keep It Varied
🍳
Scrambled
4 eggs + Knorr drops while cooking. Fast. Works with any sauce.
⚪
Boiled
Batch boil 3–4. Eat cold with pepper sauce. No cooking during the day.
🟡
Fried
2 eggs fried in minimal oil. Add soy sauce or pepper sauce at the end.
🥘
Omelette
3 eggs with tomato, onion, pepper. Filling. Masks the egg taste best.
